# User Roles

## Pet Owner
Full access to their own pets, records, documents, reminders, and sharing controls.

## Vet / Clinic User
Access to approved pet medical and care information based on owner permissions.

## Pet Sitter / Caregiver
Access to approved care information, feeding instructions, behaviour notes, and limited medication details where allowed.

## Emergency Viewer
Read-only access to emergency-safe information such as allergies, urgent notes, and owner contact details.

## Admin
Platform management access for users, pets, reminders, support, and monitoring.

## Super Admin
Highest-level platform control for admin management, settings, permissions, and audit review.
